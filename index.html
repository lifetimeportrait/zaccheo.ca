01 / THE ARTISTBEHIND THE ART

A PRACTICE IN PROCESS

Finding formin the feeling.

Rino Zaccheo is a Montreal-based abstract expressionist painter whose work explores the energy and emotion of the unplanned.

With a background in interior design, Zaccheo first turned to painting to create art for the spaces he shaped. What began with still lifes and figures gradually opened into a more instinctive, abstract language, informed by artists such as Jackson Pollock and Damien Hirst.

Working from his home studio in Montréal’s West Island, he creates expressive works held in private and corporate collections. Each painting is an invitation to look beyond the surface — and find something personal in the process.
